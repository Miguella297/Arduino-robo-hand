
## August 17, 2026 — 30 minutes in

First time ever using an Arduino Uno. Spent a lot of time wrapping my head around how to properly connect jumper wires to GND, 5V, and the specialized digital input/output pins. Was working on connecting 2 separate servos to the Arduino Uno, powered from my computer. Wrote the code in the Arduino IDE editor.

## August 17, 2026 — 1 hour in

Connected the Arduino Uno to my laptop and began experimenting with and altering the code to make the servo horns move differently. Hadn't fully thought out the mechanical design yet for how the servos would move the forearm and fingers of the cardboard hand. Was thinking a full 180-degree rotational loop would be necessary for the fingers, arm, or wrist to move back and forth fully. Had not yet begun assembling the cardboard arm itself at this point.

## August 17, 2026 — 1 hour 30 minutes in

After formatting the code so the Arduino Uno moved the servos exactly how I wanted, I began gathering the equipment needed to build the cardboard hand — popsicle sticks, cardboard boxes, elastic bands, glue sticks, and more. Slowly started sketching a mental image of how to carve out the hand and how big I wanted it to be.

## August 17, 2026 — 3 hours in

After gathering all the equipment, I began assembling the cardboard arm piece by piece. Sketched the hand shape and cut it out of a cardboard box. Started testing whether the cardboard was light enough to be easily moved by a string attached to the servo as it rotated back and forth.

## August 18, 2026 — 5 hours in

Work continued into a second day. Began applying my own designs to the hand, including cutting out a bicep piece where the forearm extension would connect. Everything was going to plan. Had to reconnect the servos occasionally after they disconnected from the Arduino Uno a few times. Enjoyed the work but started feeling somewhat exhausted over time, especially with other projects waiting to be worked on. Noticed I always feel more enthusiastic at the start of a work session than toward the end — but still proud of how the mechanical hand was turning out and confident it was heading toward success.

## August 18, 2026 — 6 hours 30 minutes in

Seriously considered how to connect the servos to the arm. Wanted to avoid hot-gluing directly onto the horn, worried it would ruin the servo. Originally tried attaching a popsicle stick to the horn with an elastic band, connected to the hand by a string — abandoned that approach because the bands wouldn't hold the popsicle stick still against the horn. Ended up applying hot glue directly to the horn, and it held up fine without damaging the servo.

## August 18, 2026 — 7 hours 30 minutes in

Started attaching rope to the fingers and forearm of the cardboard hand. Spent a lot of time trying to get the servo horn to lift the forearm, but it wasn't strong enough — had to think of a workaround.

## August 19, 2026 — 9 hours 43 minutes in (12 hours 20 minutes total including software work)

Project complete. Ended up attaching the first servo to move the wrist instead of the forearm, since the forearm proved too heavy. Added more art/design details to the hand and spent time fine-tuning the Arduino code to adjust the servo horn movements and the mechanism as a whole.
